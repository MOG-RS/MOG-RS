🧠 TENMO: Understanding Neuromyelitis Optica

A clear and practical guide for patients and families

📋 What is TENMO?

  • An inflammatory disorder of the central nervous system primarily affecting the optic nerves and spinal cord.
  • The immune system produces AQP4 antibodies, which damage myelin and nerve fibers.
  • Causes inflammation that can affect vision and movement.

👥 Who is Affected?

  • Mostly occurs in adults, more commonly in women.
  • It is not inherited or contagious.
  • The exact cause is often unknown; sometimes it occurs after infections.

⚠️ Main Symptoms

👁️

Optic Neuritis

Vision loss, eye pain, blurred or faded vision

🦵

Myelitis

Weakness in arms or legs, tingling, numbness, bladder or bowel problems

⚡

Other Symptoms

Neuropathic pain, fatigue, difficulty walking

🔍 Comparison with MOGAD and MS

Feature TENMO MOGAD MS

🔬 Diagnosis

💉

Blood Test

For AQP4 antibodies

🧲

MRI

Brain, optic nerves, and spinal cord

🩺

Lumbar Puncture

If needed

👨‍⚕️

Specialist

Neurologist in neuroimmunology

💊 Treatment

During Attacks

Intravenous steroids; if insufficient, plasma exchange or IV immunoglobulins

Relapse Prevention (Approved Treatments)

Eculizumab

Ravulizumab

Inebilizumab

Satralizumab

Rituximab

Traditional immunosuppressive therapies

As determined by the treating physician

📊 Prognosis

  • The disease can be recurrent, and attacks may leave lasting effects.
  • With rehabilitation and personalized treatment, long-term impact can be minimized, helping patients maintain a good quality of life.

💡 Practical Tips

🚨
Recognize Warning Signs: Changes in vision, weakness, tingling, bladder or bowel problems
📅
Follow Treatment: Take prescribed medications and attend regular medical check-ups
🏃
Stay Active: Engage in adapted exercise and get enough sleep
🛡️
Prevent Infections: Take precautions to stay healthy
❤️
Emotional Support: Psychological support is important for wellbeing and adaptation

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does TENMO last forever?

It is a chronic condition, but with proper treatment, it can be managed, allowing a good quality of life.

Can I exercise?

Yes, safely, if adapted to your abilities.

Is pregnancy possible?

With specialist care, most pregnancies proceed normally.
